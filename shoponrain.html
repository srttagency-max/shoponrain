FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. You get 7 days free with full access to build and preview your store.

Do you take a commission on my sales?

No. You connect your own payment gateway and customers pay you directly. You only pay the flat subscription.

Where do I buy my domain?

Anywhere you like. Buy it from any registrar, then add the DNS records from your dashboard. We handle verification and SSL.

Can I sell products and take bookings together?

Yes. Choose “Products & bookable services” when you register.

Can I change my template later?

Yes. Switch templates any time — your products and content stay in place.

Is my store secure?

Every store gets free SSL, two-factor login, email verification and spam protection by default.
